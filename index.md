## Technical Interests

### Generative art (Not including AI)

As a visual artist, I am always looking for a new medium or inspiration to work with. The visuals produced by Brownian motion and iterated function systems (IFS) were life-changing in this regard. Ever since I first discovered computer-generated fractals, I have been utterly enraptured with the idea of taking mathematical process and incorporating them into my art. It represents a complete upheaval of my usual artistic process, and a near-perfect example of the intersection between art and science.

### Networking and cybersecurity

Initially born of a desire to have more personal control and understanding of my home network, my interest in **[creating and managing networks, self-hosting, system administration, and improving security]** has absolutely blossomed over the past 3 years. I have created a personal website (which is unfortunately no longer active for financial reasons), a web server capable of streaming media and file hosting, and a self-hosted VPN, among other projects.

### Operating system development (OSDev)

I have wanted to build my own operating system for a very long time, and it has been a long-term goal of mine to be able to approach this task. So far, I have built the Linux kernel from scratch and written a very rudimentary EFI executable, but I am continuing to work towards my ultimate goal of a microkernel capable of supporting a graphical desktop environment.
